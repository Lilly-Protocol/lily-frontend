# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

Lily Frontend is a contributor-focused project. We expect everyone who participates in issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and project spaces to help maintain a respectful, practical, and harassment-free environment.

## Expected Behavior

Examples of behavior that supports a healthy community include:

- using welcoming and inclusive language
- respecting different experience levels, viewpoints, and constraints
- giving feedback that is specific, constructive, and focused on the work
- accepting responsibility for mistakes and correcting them promptly
- prioritizing what is best for the project and its users

## Unacceptable Behavior

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- harassment, intimidation, stalking, or sustained disruption
- insults, demeaning comments, or personal attacks
- sexualized language or imagery in project spaces
- publishing private information without explicit permission
- abuse of project processes, maintainer time, or contributor trust

## Reporting

Report Code of Conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers through the Lily Protocol contact channel listed on the project website or the GitHub organization profile. Include relevant links, screenshots, dates, and a concise description of what happened when it is safe to do so.

Do not open a public issue for sensitive conduct reports. Maintainers should acknowledge reports, review available context, protect the reporter's privacy, and take proportionate action.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may take any action they determine is appropriate and proportionate, including clarifying expectations, removing comments, closing threads, limiting participation, or banning contributors from project spaces.

Enforcement decisions should consider the impact on the community, the safety of affected people, and the need to keep project work constructive.
